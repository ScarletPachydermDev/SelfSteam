# Supported

SelfSteam runs on x86_64 (x64) only: the Steam Deck, the Steam Machine, and
x86_64 PCs with a Steam gamescope session. The Flatpak is built for x86_64,
and so are the emulators and AppImages it installs.

## Emulators tab

Installed from Flathub, or as AppImages from their developers:

Azahar, BigPEmu, Cemu, Dolphin, DuckStation, Eden, Flycast, gopher64,
M64Py, melonDS, PCSX2, Play!, PPSSPP, Rosalie's Mupen GUI, RPCS3, Ryubing
(and Ryubing Canary), shadPS4, Vita3K, Wheel Wizard, xemu, Xenia Canary.

Every one of them can use [Preflight](https://github.com/ScarletPachydermDev/Preflight)
for controllers.
